> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a new three-panel panoramic banner for an engineering portfolio website, displayed at the same width as the text content. Create ONE combined image in a very wide 3:1 landscape format, ideally 3072 x 1024. Three equally sized vertical photographic panels, aligned edge to edge with very thin warm-ivory separators; no captions or text overlays. LEFT panel: a precise industrial robotic arm and gripper handling a small machined component on a clean workbench, with a depth-camera sensor and organized industrial automation setting; believable mechanics. CENTER panel: AI and materials research workstation, showing an elegant luminous atomic crystal lattice visualization on a physical monitor, with subtle laboratory/data-compute equipment in the background; no readable interface text and no fake charts. RIGHT panel: close-up of an embedded electronics and wearable-computing prototype on a bench, a compact circuit board, sensor module, small optical heads-up-display component, and carefully machined housing; credible physical scale and construction. Shared photographic language: sophisticated contemporary editorial technology photography, natural directional light, deep teal/slate shadows, brushed silver materials, warm off-white highlights and restrained copper details, realistic textures and optical depth, professional rather than glossy sci-fi. Keep important subjects in the central vertical band so the panorama remains legible at a modest banner height. Show technology concepts, not claimed photographs of the portfolio owner's actual projects. No people, no weapons, no logos, no watermarks, no words, no numbers, no flowchart boxes, no floating holograms. The panels should be visually distinct scenes yet feel like a coherent triptych.
